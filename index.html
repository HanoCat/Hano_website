About

Hey… I'm HANO. Welcome to my corner of the internet! I’m driven by curiosity about how people interact with space. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, crowd-aware design, and reinforcement learning, where I explore how computational methods can support better, more human-centered environments. I enjoy writing, reading, creating tools, asking hard questions, and learning in public. This website is my space to share my work, thoughts, and ongoing explorations.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Leeds. I hold a Master’s degree in Software Engineering from King Saud University and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Princess Nourah University. Prior to my current role, I worked at Princess Nourah University as a Teaching Assistant and as Project Manager for the Quality System.
